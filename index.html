Catalogue Raisonné

NFTs & Digital Works

This section documents NFT projects, digital collectibles, readable digital comics, music-related releases, and collaborative blockchain projects associated with Ron English. Each card opens a project page where available; individual work pages provide catalogue metadata, images, and source links.

Ron English Essential NFT Collection

An early Nifty Gateway release presenting a concise cross-section of Ron English's Popaganda imagery, from Mona Lisa Grin and Uncle Scam to Cereal Killers and Delusionville characters.

Ron English Return

A July 2021 Nifty Gateway follow-up drop extending Delusionville with Fallah Sheep, Montana Mouse, Punk Skunk, Forlorn Unicorn, Urban Bigfoot, Rabbbit Coin, Snake, and Riff Raff.

Light Cult Crypto Club

A large-scale Ron English NFT collection built around the light bulb as a symbol of ideas, translating his Popaganda universe into thousands of character-driven digital works.

Ron English's Area 54 - Cards / Packs

An Ethereum trading-card NFT project expanding Ron English's Area 54 universe through collectible character cards and pack-based releases.

The Grand Delusion Part 1

A limited-edition NFT drop tied to Ron English's Delusionville mythology, centering character-driven digital works such as Potato Rabbit, Scrap Ard, Kursed Zephyr, and Poultry Rex.

Ron English's Cereal Killers

Ron English's largest VeVe digital collectible family, beginning with the June 2021 Cereal Killers launch and expanding through later character drops, gold variants, special digital prints, and diorama releases.

Rabbit Grin Phygital Collectibles

A VeVe phygital release translating Ron English's Rabbit Grin imagery into paired physical-and-digital collectibles, including Chrome Rabbit Grin and Gold Rabbit Grin.

Ukraine Relief Project

A charity-oriented VeVe release featuring Grade School Guernica 2022 and Sunflower Grin - Special Mission, with first-sale net proceeds directed to Ukrainian refugee relief.

David Warhol / Young Warhol

A VeVe Artworks release centered on David Warhol, a 1-of-1 Artist Proof sold through silent auction, with Young Warhol documented as a related promotional airdrop for auction participants.

See, Hear and Speak More Evil Monkeys

A VeVe Popaganda digital collectible series reworking the see, hear, and speak no evil motif through Ron English's darker public-discourse imagery.

Mogul Grin

A VeVe Popaganda collectible family centered on Mogul Grin, including the original Series 1 release and later Mogul Grin Board Game releases.

Big Top Popaganda #1

A readable VeVe digital comic containing origin material for MC Supersized and Orion the Obese Alien, plus parody-ad and Popaganda content.

Area 54 #1

A readable VeVe digital comic presenting the origin story of Orion the Alien and MC Supersized, catalogued separately from the Area 54 Cards / Packs project.

Delusionville #1

A readable VeVe digital comic introducing Ron English's upside-down Rabbbithole world of Delusionville, inhabited by Rabbbits, wolves, unicorns, and other figures from his surreal narrative universe.

Fauxlosophy #1

A readable VeVe digital comic built around Ron English's Fauxlosophy quotes and aphorisms, including the line: If you believe, it doesn't have to be true.

VeVeVibes: The Rabbbits Jukebox / Delusionville Records

A VeVeVibes music-and-digital-collectible project connecting Ron English's Delusionville universe with original songs by The Rabbbits.

Califari Strain Art Trading Card Series: Grand Daddy Purple

An animated NFT trading-card adaptation of Ron English's Grand Daddy Purple strain artwork for Califari, released as part of the platform's cannabis strain-art NFT program.

A Tune from the Tomb / Jeremiah Grin

A Daniel Johnston x Ron English x Clutter Studios NFT/music project built from a rediscovered song English and Johnston wrote years earlier, paired with Jeremiah Grin NFT editions.

Time To Be Happy 00:01

A Benzi project curated by gmoney in which antique timepieces were transformed into artist-restyled physical works paired with 1-of-1 NFTs; Ron English was one of the participating artists.
