VeVe: Ukraine Relief Project

A charity-oriented VeVe release featuring Ron English's Grade School Guernica 2022 and Sunflower Grin - Special Mission. VeVe described the project as a benefit drop supporting Ukrainian refugee relief through the New Zealand Red Cross and the International Committee of the Red Cross.
