VeVe: Ron English’s Cereal Killers

Ron English’s Cereal Killers became one of his largest VeVe digital collectible families, beginning with the June 2021 Series 1 release and expanding through later character drops, gold and collector-gift variants, special digital prints, and display-environment releases.

Series 1

Ron English’s first VeVe Cereal Killers release, issued on June 10, 2021.

Fat Tony Cap'n Cornstarch Honey Butt the Obese Count Calorie

Series 2

The second Cereal Killers VeVe release, expanding the breakfast-table cast with four additional characters.

Two Ton Sam Franken Fat Sugar Diabetic Bear Yucky Charmer

Series 3

The third Cereal Killers VeVe release, completing the original twelve-piece character sequence.

Smack, Crack & Pot Drug 'em Killfrog Qhrist Tricky the Obese Bunny

Special Releases

Special Cereal Killers artwork and digital-print releases connected to the Last Fat Breakfast image.

Last Fat Breakfast

Gold and Special Editions

Gold and collector-gift variants extending the Cereal Killers VeVe group beyond the original character drops.

Fat Tony - Gold Franken Fat - Gold/Silver Tricky the Obese Bunny - Gold Yucky Charmer - Gold

Series 4

A later VeVe Cereal Killers blind-box release pairing Poo Fairy with Coco Paunch.

Poo Fairy Coco Paunch

Diorama

A display-environment release connected to Ron English’s Cereal Killers and Last Supper imagery.

The Last Supper Diorama
