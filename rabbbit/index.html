VeVe: Rabbit Grin Phygital Collectibles

A VeVe phygital / digital sculpture project based on Ron English’s Rabbit Grin imagery. Early coverage described a digital-and-physical Rabbbit Grin release, while VeVe later documented Chrome Rabbit Grin and Gold Rabbit Grin as Secret Rare digital collectibles in the Rabbit Grin series.
